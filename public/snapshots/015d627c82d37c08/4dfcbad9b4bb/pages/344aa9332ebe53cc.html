Pharloom Bay

Pharloom Bay was an area removed in 2022. Unlike the other areas on this site, there are few traces of it in the current version of the game. This may be because it was never outright removed; the teaser for the Sea of Sorrow DLC planned for 2026 has strong evidence that the area will, at least in some form, return.

It had a village area and its biggest mechanic is the submersible, which would have allowed you to access other, smaller areas. One of these areas, an area known as "Lab", was also shown in the teaser for Sea of Sorrow.
