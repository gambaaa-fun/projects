Hunter's Nest

Just as with most of the other areas mentioned on this site, Hunter's Nest was an area that was reworked in 2021. This rework was unique to the other areas, as instead of being removed or drastically changed, it was reworked into the much smaller Hunter's March.

The area itself had multiple versions, starting as a cave system full of ant mounds, before being redesigned into an area with more plants and resembling Hunter's March far more. It had multiple subareas, such as the Beastmaster's den and the opera room, though the latter was moved to a separate area in 2019.

The quality of the map shown is quite low, but this is what the area looked like in 2021.
