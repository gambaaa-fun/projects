Red Coral Gorge

The Red Coral Gorge was a major area in Hollow Knight: Silksong. Before being reworked into the Sands of Karak and Blasted steps areas, it went through multiple iterations. Although the full history is not known, as Team Cherry did not always keep their development maps up to date, what is known is that the earlier and later versions were tonally different, with the later versions adopting a less lush look, likely to reflect the shift into a darker Pharloom overall.

Development on the first iteration of the area started somewhere around 2017 and continued all the way until 2019. This version contained only one main area, a lush coral cavern which was at the time likely called "Pink Coral Forest" or just "Coral Forest". Following 2019, this area was redesigned into the Coral Steps, a subarea of the Red Coral Gorge as a whole.

The final iteration of the area came in 2021, and this version is what the stitched map is for. As mentioned before, it is not 100% accurate due to Team Cherry not updating the maps. This version contained three main subareas; the Coral River, Coral Steps, and Voltnest.
The Coral Steps were a less vibrant area that had stone manmade structures and a minor amount of coral. This area would be reworked into the Blasted Steps.
The Coral River was a cavernous area containing far more coral. It also housed the Coral Tower. This area was reworked into the Sands of Karak.
Voltnest is an area in the final game, although it was reduced in size.
