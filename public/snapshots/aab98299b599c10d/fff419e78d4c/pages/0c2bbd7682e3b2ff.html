Buyer's Guide

Everything you need to choose the right greenhouse and accessories for your garden.

1. Choose your greenhouse type

Polycarbonate

Lightweight, excellent insulation, and shatter-resistant. Ideal for year-round growing and harsh weather. Best value for most home gardeners.

Browse polycarbonate

Glass

Classic appearance with maximum light transmission. Perfect if you want a traditional look and premium ventilation options.

Browse glass

Lean-to

Attaches to a wall to save space. Great for patios, narrow plots, and gardeners who want easy access from the house.

Browse lean-to

Mini & cold frames

Compact protection for seedlings and early crops. Affordable entry point before upgrading to a full greenhouse.

Browse mini & cold frames

2. Size and placement

  • Measure your space — Allow at least 60 cm clearance around the structure for maintenance and airflow.
  • Check sun exposure — South-facing sites receive the most light; avoid heavy shade from trees or buildings.
  • Prepare a level base — A concrete slab, paving, or compacted gravel base keeps the frame stable and doors aligned.
  • Plan for wind and snow — Anchor larger models; our heavy-duty profiles are built for demanding conditions.

3. Essential accessories

Ventilation, staging, and shading make a big difference to temperature control and plant health. We recommend starting with an auto vent opener and shelving if you are new to greenhouse growing.
