OUR STORY

HOW IT ALL STARTED

Glowride started as a small group of friends who simply loved to skate. Over the years our community grew, and what began as casual sessions evolved into organized training, trips, and events across the region. Today we continue to build an open and welcoming space for anyone who wants to roll with us — whether you're a seasoned freeskater or just getting started.
