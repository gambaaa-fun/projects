Trip to Osečná

About the event

Our trip to Osečná was one of the most memorable sessions of the season. The route took us through the beautiful Liberec Region, with smooth roads and stunning scenery all the way. Skaters of all levels joined in, making it a perfect mix of sport and adventure. We stopped for a picnic halfway through, caught up, shared tricks, and made the most of the summer weather. It's exactly the kind of day that reminds us why we started skating in the first place.
