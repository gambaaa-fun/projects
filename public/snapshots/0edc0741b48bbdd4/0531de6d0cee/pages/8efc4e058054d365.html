Event in Sokolovna

About the event

Our event at Sokolovna brought together skaters from around the region for a day of inline skating, music, and great company. The hall provided plenty of room to skate freely, try new tricks, and connect with other enthusiasts. It was a fun, relaxed atmosphere where beginners and experienced skaters alike could enjoy the sport we all love.
