What is Lethal Company?

Lethal Company is a co-op horror game where you play as a contracted worker for the mysterious "Company". Your job is to collect scrap from abandoned, industrialized moons to meet a profit quota.

Failure to meet the quota results in... termination. Success means harder quotas and more dangerous moons.

The Gameplay Loop

  • 1. Land: Choose a moon and land your ship.
  • 2. Scavenge: Enter the facility and find valuable scrap.
  • 3. Survive: Avoid monsters, traps, and environmental hazards.
  • 4. Extract: Return to the ship with your loot before midnight.
  • 5. Profit: Sell your scrap at the Company Building to meet the quota.
